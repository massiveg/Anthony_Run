How to Play

  • Move: WASD / Arrow Keys, or touch joystick (left half of screen).
  • Combat: Your weapon fires automatically at the nearest enemy.
  • Endless horde: Enemies never stop spawning. Spawn rate, count, strength and enemy types scale with your level.
  • Upgrades: Every level-up pauses the game and offers a Buff & Nerf upgrade (click or press 1/2/3).
  • Waves: A new wave begins every 45 seconds and makes enemies tougher.
  • Zoom: Mouse wheel, + / - keys, on-screen buttons, or pinch with two fingers.
